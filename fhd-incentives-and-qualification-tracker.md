## Best Tracking Approach

Track only the metrics that decide qualification:

- Production / NAP written.
- Applications submitted.
- Appointments sat.
- Appointments booked.
- Leads contacted.
- Average production or NAP per app.
- Close rate: apps submitted divided by appointments sat.
- Show rate: appointments sat divided by appointments booked.
- Contact to appointment rate: appointments booked divided by leads contacted.

Everything else is secondary unless it directly improves one of those metrics.

## Weekly Review Rule

Every weekly update should answer these questions:

1. What was actual production / NAP this week?
2. What is cumulative production / NAP versus target?
3. What is the new required weekly pace for the remaining weeks?
4. Which bottleneck is first: lead volume, booking rate, show rate, close rate, or average case size?
5. What is the next week's minimum, target, and PR attempt?

## Status Rules

- Green: cumulative actual is at least 100% of cumulative target.
- Yellow: cumulative actual is 85% to 99% of cumulative target.
- Red: cumulative actual is below 85% of cumulative target.

## Diagnostic Priority

If behind pace, diagnose in this order:

1. Average production / NAP per app is too low.
2. Close rate from sit to app is too low.
3. Show rate from booked to sat is too low.
4. Not enough appointments are booked.
5. Not enough leads are contacted.

## Current Recommendation

For August 2026, do not use `$27,500/month` as the only motivational target. Keep it visible as the qualification pace, but run a PR ladder:

- Minimum standard: beat `$3,000`.
- Solid month: `$5,000`.
- Strong PR: `$7,500`.
- Breakout month: `$10,000+`.
- Qualification pace: `$27,500`.

The goal is to raise the floor first, then push the ceiling. Weekly stats should decide whether the bottleneck is activity volume, conversion, or case size.
